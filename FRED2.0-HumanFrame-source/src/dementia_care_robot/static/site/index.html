Why we’re here

Robotics should meet
people where they are.

Too much technology asks people to adapt to it. We believe the best robots will do the opposite—understanding our spaces, respecting our routines, and supporting the moments that matter.

Human Frame Robotics is creating an accessible framework that others can extend for meaningful real-world applications.

A few things to know

Your questions,
answered openly.

Can I use the framework today?

Not yet. The Human Frame is in development. We have not announced general availability and are not taking orders or deposits.

Is the robot in the image a working prototype?

The hero image is an AI-generated concept illustration. It communicates our design direction and does not show a finished product or verified capabilities.

What can be built with it?

We see potential across research, education, home interaction, hospitality, and care. Dementia support remains one meaningful future application, rather than the framework’s sole focus.

Who would you like to hear from?

Robotics developers, researchers, product teams, educators, care organizations, and potential technical partners. Tell us what you want to build or which part of the framework you would like to explore.

Will the framework be open source?

We are still evaluating the right model for hardware, software, developer access, and commercial use. Early partner conversations will help shape that decision.
